## Where the 134,216 comes from — all of it the workbook's

1. **Two 800 A withdrawable motorised ACBs** (`3WJ1108-2AE12-4DD0-Z F40+S55+T40`): workbook
   330,432 each, catalogue 1,398.72 EUR × 200 = 279,744. Difference 101,376. The workbook figure
   is a stale or hand-adjusted price (reference §6.2).
2. **LOGO controller**: workbook 60,000, catalogue 154 EUR × 200 = 30,800. Difference 29,200.
3. **Rounded KES prices on the APFC capacitors and contactors** (ZJE…, CSTC…, TC1D…): the
   workbook rounds to the nearest 10 or 50 KES; net +810.
4. **Small drifts**: 6 A SP MCB 308 vs 246 (× 22 = 1,364), LM1340 meter 12,500 vs 11,500 (× 3
   = 3,000), 1600 A ACB 217,330 vs 217,322, 250 A MCCB 19,905 vs 19,904, 160 A MCCB 14,200 vs
   14,176.
5. **Busbar matches exactly**: the seed's kg per metre is the catalogue price ÷ 15, and
   15 EUR/kg × 200 = the workbook's 3,000 KES/kg.
6. **Ten lines are not in the catalogue** and are typed in at the workbook price: SYNCHRO CHECK
   RELAYS, CONTROLS, the Rishabh CTs (1600/5 and 800/5 at three prices), `3WJ1108-2AE02-1AD1`
   (a typo for `…-1AD0`, which is in the catalogue at 171,562), timers and relays, the
   FK5526-230 filter fan. Reference §6.3 lists them as parts to add to the catalogue.

Acceptance therefore reads: **rebuild Option 1 from the seed; material must equal 3,622,781.80
to the cent with the three category figures above; the margin, rounding and VAT steps must
reproduce the workbook's 5,784,800 / 925,568.00 / 6,710,368.00 exactly.**
