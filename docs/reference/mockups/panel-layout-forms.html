Form of separation — the same MCCB section as 2B, 3B and 4B
The form chosen on the panel adds partitions and shrouds to the drawing and drives the enclosure uplift (decision 1)
Form 2B
Busbars separated from the functional units. Units share the space; terminals open.
Uplift: none (catalogue cubicle)
Form 3B
Each functional unit in its own compartment; outgoing terminals not separated from each other.
Uplift: +3 partitions, shrouds
Form 4B
As 3B, plus each unit's terminals in their own compartment in the cable alley.
Uplift: +6 partitions, shrouds, glands
What the app does with the form
Draws the partitions (thick black lines) so the GA sketch matches the offer wording in Annexure I.
Counts partitions and shrouds per section and feeds the enclosure uplift rule — the rule you still have to define (decision 1). Once defined, the uplift line in the costing is calculated from the drawing, not typed.
Adds the extra plate height each partition needs to the fit check, so a section that fits as 2B can correctly fail as 4B.
